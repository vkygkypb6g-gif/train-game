    How to play

    This is fake money — you start with $1,000. Place a bet, then tap one of the three tracks. A train comes down a random track from the tunnels. If it isn’t yours, you clear the pass and the multiplier climbs (1.46× each clear). Your chicken stays on the crossing and only steps sideways when you pick a track. Cash out whenever you like, or keep going. Get hit and the bet is gone. There is a small house edge, about 3% per clear. Auto mode can cash out after a set number of clears.

    Out of cash

    You’re out of fake cash. Reset the bankroll to $1,000 and try the tunnels again.
