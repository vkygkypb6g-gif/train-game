    How to play

    This is fake money — you start with $1,000. Step onto the tracks and tap one of the three tunnels. A train bursts out of one random tunnel and races toward you. If it isn’t yours, you survive and the multiplier goes up (1.46x each clear). Tap another track to keep going, or cash out and take your bet times the current multiplier. Get hit and the bet is gone — the mole just gets a cartoony bonk. There is a small house edge, about 3% per clear.

    Stranded at the platform

    You’re out of fake cash. Reset the bankroll to $1,000 and try the tunnels again.
