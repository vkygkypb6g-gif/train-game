    How to play

    This is fake money — you start with $1,000. Step onto the tracks and pick a tunnel. A train bursts out of one random tunnel. If it isn’t yours, you survive and the multiplier goes up. Pick again to keep climbing, or cash out and take your bet times the current multiplier. Get hit and the bet is gone — cartoony, not gory. More tracks means a safer clear but a smaller step up. The payout is tuned with a small house edge, about 3% per clear.

    Stranded at the platform

    You’re out of fake cash. Reset the bankroll to $1,000 and try the tunnels again.
